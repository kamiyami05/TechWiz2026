Module 1

Foundations of Personal Budgeting

Understand where money flows, how fixed and variable expenses differ, and why small planning decisions create long-term stability.

💵

Income Inflow

Allowances, campus scholarships, paid internships, or part-time wages. Knowing your true net monthly cash flow is Step 1.

🔒

Fixed Expenses

Predictable costs that don't fluctuate month-to-month: student housing rent, Wi-Fi, tuition payments, and transit passes.

🌊

Variable Expenses

Spending that varies based on behavior: groceries, dining out, entertainment, laundry, and discretionary purchases.

Module 2

Needs vs. Wants Classification Game

Test your spending discernment! Decide whether each realistic student purchase represents a non-negotiable Need or a flexible Want.

Interactive Challenge Score: 0 / 6
💡 The 3-Question Purchase Filter:
  1. Would my health, safety, or academic performance suffer without this item? (If No → Want)
  2. Can I borrow it, rent it, or buy it secondhand? (If Yes → Save money)
  3. Can I wait 48 hours before purchasing? (80% of impulse wants disappear after 48 hours!)
